# T2 Paine 2022 vs 2023 Comparison v0

> Case: 派能能源/派能科技 2022 户储暴增期 vs 2023 暴跌期  
> Purpose: Narrow comparison memo supporting `t2_paine_reversal_findings_v0.md`  
> Evidence pull date: 2026-06-11

## Question

派能能源在 2022 户储暴增期和 2023 暴跌期，卖方研报叙事是否发生反向解释？

## Comparison

| Dimension | 2022 / early 2023 boom narrative | 2023 downturn narrative | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core driver | 欧洲能源危机、高电价、能源自主可控、政策支持、户储经济性。 | 欧洲户储渠道库存高企、经销商去库、需求增速阶段性放缓。 | Same market moved from "demand-pull growth" to "inventory-cycle drag." |
| Sell-side examples | 东吴证券 2022-04-27: 海外居民电价上涨 + 政策驱动，预计 2022 出货超 3GWh、2023 持续高增。华福证券 2023-04-10: 俄乌冲突只是催化剂，核心是能源安全和经济性，预计 2023 销量 7.5GWh。 | 华福证券 2023-10-31: Q3 出货约 300MWh，前三季度 1.7GWh，同比 -23%，主要受欧洲户储整体去库存影响。东吴证券 2024-04-12: 2023 出货 1.9GWh，同比 -47%，欧洲经销商持续去库。 | Yes. Sell-side explanation flips at the channel/shipments layer. |
| Company actuals | 2022 营收 60.13 亿元，同比 +191.55%；归母净利 12.73 亿元，同比 +302.53%；储能系统销售量 3505.83MWh。 | 2023 营收 32.99 亿元，同比 -45.13%；归母净利 5.16 亿元，同比 -59.49%；软包电池销量 1874.53MWh，同比 -46.92%。 | Actual performance confirms boom-to-bust baseline. |
| Company attribution | 2022 年报: 全球储能市场需求快速增长；欧洲能源供应短缺、市场需求旺盛；公司产能瓶颈缓解，产能利用率较高。 | 2023 年报: 宏观环境、补贴退坡、海外下游企业去库存影响，家储需求增速阶段性放缓；海外下游渠道商库存高企，海外家储持续处于去库阶段。 | Official filings corroborate the narrative shift. |
| Conflict source | Shipments and revenues looked like strong structural demand in 2022. | 2024 IR record reframes 2022-2024 as one cycle: 2023 Q1 supply-chain panic created high inventory; 2023 industry shipments fell while European installations were relatively steady. | The key conflict is shipment demand vs terminal installation demand. |

## Evidence-backed Verdict

Yes. The evidence supports a narrow but material reverse explanation:

1. 2022/early-2023 sell-side reports interpreted the European residential storage boom as durable demand driven by energy security, high electricity prices, economics, and policy.
2. By 2023 Q3, sell-side reports explained weak shipments and earnings through European residential storage destocking and channel inventory.
3. Company filings support the same shift: 2022 was demand/scale expansion; 2023 was overseas downstream inventory + subsidy/macro pressure + household-storage sales decline.
4. The failure attribution should be assigned to channel inventory and shipment timing, not to a simple collapse in European end-user installation demand.
